# MyEverFind

A discovery engine for vintage and used music gear. Find guitars, synthesizers, drums, studio equipment, and more across eBay, Reverb, and other platforms—all in one place.

**Built for gear obsessives by gear obsessives.**

## Features

### 🔍 Smart Search & Browse
- Search across multiple marketplaces simultaneously
- Filter by instrument type, price range, condition, and era
- AI-powered deal scoring tells you if a listing is a steal or overpriced

### 🎲 Discover Hidden Gems
- **Random Gem Finder** — Stumble upon unexpected treasures
- **Find Your Vibe** — Quick quiz to personalize recommendations based on your taste
- **Rare Finds** — Alerts when historically significant gear appears
- **Just Dropped** — See the newest listings before they're gone

### 📚 Curated Collections
- Expert-picked themed collections ("Budget Blues Rig", "Home Studio Essentials")
- Hand-curated drops instead of algorithmic spam
- Learn why gear matters through expert commentary

### 🏆 Build Your Collection
- Save gear to your personal wishlist
- Showcase your collection to the community
- See what other collectors are building
- Price drop alerts on saved items

### 📈 Market Trends & Insights
- Price history charts for vintage gear
- What's trending in the community
- Market insights (what's appreciating, what's cooling off)
- Expert gear guides and collector stories

### 🔔 Smart Notifications
- Price drop alerts with urgency signals
- Rare find notifications when holy grail items appear
- Market insights and curated collection updates
- Build a daily discovery habit with streak tracking

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — Semantic markup
- **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive design system
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — No build step, no dependencies
- **SVG** — Custom logo and charts

## Getting Started

1. Clone the repo
2. Open `index.html` in a web browser
3. Start exploring!

No build step, no backend needed — everything is self-contained.

## How It Works

**The Core Value Prop:**
- **Aggregation** — We pull listings from eBay, Reverb, and other sources so you don't have to hunt manually
- **AI Intelligence** — Smart parsing of condition descriptions, price history tracking, and deal detection
- **Curation** — Expert picks and themed collections feel more trustworthy than pure algorithms
- **Community** — Discover what collectors like you are hunting and showcase your taste
- **Serendipity** — Built-in discovery tools help you find gear you didn't know you wanted

## Pages

- **Home** — Hero with search and sample results
- **Discover** — Random gem finder, vibe quiz, rare finds, just dropped

- **Explore** — Advanced filtering by instrument, price, condition, era
- **Trending** — Market insights, price trends, community picks
